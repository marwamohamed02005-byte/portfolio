About Me

Design Philosophy & Background

I'm a graphic designer focused on logo design, visual identity, and creative brand applications. I enjoy developing visual directions that reflect a project's personality and translating ideas into clear, cohesive design solutions.

Visual identity is more than surface aesthetics—it is the strategic visual voice through which a business or product connects with its audience. My passion lies in distilling the core essence of each project into an enduring visual system that communicates with clarity, emotion, and aesthetic distinction.

Download CV

My Design Approach

My design process is built around harmonizing five fundamental pillars:

  • 01 Visual Appeal: Creating refined, elegant aesthetics that elevate perceived value.
  • 02 Clarity & Communication: Ensuring the core brand message is understood instantly.
  • 03 Brand Consistency: Unifying all physical and digital collateral under one clear design rulebook.
  • 04 Typography & Composition: Meticulous attention to letterforms, spacing, and balanced layout geometry.
  • 05 Tailored Solutions: Designing specifically for the unique context and character of each brand.
